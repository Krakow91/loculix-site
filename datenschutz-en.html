2. Core principle: privacy by design

Loculix is designed as a purely local digital filing cabinet. We, the developers, have no access at any time to your documents, scans, metadata, keywords, passwords or PINs.

All essential processing steps, in particular text recognition (OCR), intelligent data extraction and categorisation, take place exclusively on your device. No sensitive document data is transmitted to our or any other external servers.

3. Data processing within the app

3.1 Camera and photos (permissions)

On first use the app requests permission to access the camera and photo library to scan or import documents. You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings.

  • Purpose: scanning, cropping and importing documents
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent by granting the permission)
  • Storage: exclusively local on the device
  • Disclosure: none, images are transmitted neither to us nor to third parties

3.2 Text recognition (OCR) and on-device intelligence

Text recognition and extraction of structured data (e.g. contract numbers, amounts, deadlines) happen directly on the device using native system frameworks (e.g. Apple Vision Framework, Google ML Kit On-Device). Your documents are not sent to the internet or third-party cloud services for this purpose.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract)

3.3 Biometric data (Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint / Windows Hello)

You can optionally protect the app with biometric authentication. Verification takes place exclusively at operating system level (e.g. Secure Enclave on Apple devices, TEE on Android). We only receive a system confirmation of success or failure (boolean value).

  • Biometric data is never read, stored or transmitted by the app
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent)

3.4 Local storage and encryption

All documents and database entries are stored locally with AES-256 encryption. The encryption key is derived from your personal password / PIN and never stored unencrypted.

3.5 End-to-end encrypted cloud sync (optional)

If you enable sync (iCloud, Google Drive, OneDrive, your own WebDAV), it happens directly between your device and your cloud provider. Data is encrypted client-side before upload (end-to-end encryption, E2EE). Your master key never leaves your device.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent by enabling sync)
  • Note: the privacy terms of the cloud providers apply to processing on their platforms: Apple, Google, Microsoft

3.6 Push notifications (optional)

The app can send notifications (e.g. reminders for contract deadlines). Permission is requested on first launch and can be revoked in your device settings at any time. Push tokens are stored only on your device.

3.7 Crash reports and diagnostics (optional)

If you have agreed in your device settings to share crash reports, anonymised reports may be forwarded to us via the store operators (Apple, Google, Microsoft). They contain no personal content of your documents, only technical error data (stack traces, device model, OS version).

5. Data processing on the website

5.1 Hosting (GitHub Pages)

Our website is provided via GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (a Microsoft company). When you visit the website, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data that your browser transmits automatically, in particular IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL, and date and time of the request.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure, stable and cost-effective provision of the website). We have no access to these server logs and do not combine them with other data.

Third-country transfer: as GitHub is based in the USA, data may be transferred to the USA. The transfer relies on the adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) or standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

5.2 Contact form and waitlist

If you send us a request via the contact form or sign up for the waitlist, the following data is processed: email address (both forms), name (contact form) and message content (contact form).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent for the waitlist).

Processing by FormSubmit.co: form data is forwarded to our email address via the service FormSubmit.co (operator: Envoke IT S.L., Madrid, Spain). FormSubmit processes and temporarily stores the data for delivery. If data is processed on servers in the USA, this is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) or, alternatively, standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). More information: FormSubmit privacy.

Data transmitted when contacting us is deleted once your request has been fully handled, unless longer retention is legally required.

5.3 Local storage

The website sets no cookies and stores no data in your browser's local storage. Language selection works via separate pages (German/English).

6. Cookies and tracking

Our website uses no cookies, no tracking pixels and no analytics tools (such as Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or similar). No profiling or behavioural tracking takes place. We use no marketing cookies or retargeting. No data is stored client-side in the browser.

7. External service providers and third parties

7.1 Fonts

This website uses only system fonts of your device. No fonts are loaded from external servers (e.g. Google Fonts). No connection to Google servers takes place.

7.2 Scripts and libraries

The website embeds no external scripts, CDNs or tracking services. All files are delivered via GitHub Pages.

7.3 FormSubmit.co

To transmit contact form and waitlist data we use FormSubmit.co (operator: Envoke IT S.L., Madrid, Spain). The service processes your data on our behalf to deliver messages to our email address.

  • Data processed: email address, name (if given), subject, message text
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR
  • Third-country transfer: data may be processed on servers in the USA; the provider ensures an adequate level of protection through standard contractual clauses
  • Privacy policy: formsubmit.co/privacy

8. Disclosure of data to third parties

Your personal data is generally not disclosed to third parties unless you have given explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), disclosure is necessary for contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), there is a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), or disclosure is necessary for legitimate interests and no overriding interests of yours exist (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

11. Your rights under the GDPR

You can exercise the following rights at any time by writing to info@loculix.de:

  • Rectification (Art. 16)
  • Erasure (Art. 17), unless a legal retention obligation applies
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability (Art. 20)
  • Objection (Art. 21), where processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f)
  • Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)) with effect for the future

12. Data security

  • TLS encryption of the website (HTTPS)
  • AES-256 encryption of all locally stored document data in the app
  • End-to-end encryption for optional cloud sync
  • Regular review of security measures

13. Children

Loculix is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that we have received personal data of a child under 16 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it without delay.

14. Changes to this privacy policy

We may amend this policy occasionally so that it always meets current legal requirements or reflects changes to our services. The new version applies to your next visit. Significant changes will be communicated on the website or in the app where possible.
