App Privacy Policy

As of April 2026

🔒 Privacy by Design: your documents stay on your device

This privacy policy applies specifically to the mobile application “Loculix” (the “app”), distributed via Google Play Store, Apple App Store, Mac App Store and Microsoft Store. It supplements the general privacy policy on our website and meets the requirements of the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the Apple App Store Review Guidelines and the GDPR.

📋 Data safety overview

This overview shows which data the app collects and shares, modelled on the Google Play Data Safety format.

✅

Optional cloud sync uses end-to-end encryption (E2EE).
✅
You can delete your data
You can delete all your data in the app at any time.
🚫

4. What the app does NOT do

  • ❌ No tracking: no tracking SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, Adjust, AppsFlyer or similar)
  • ❌ No advertising: no ads and no ad SDKs
  • ❌ No user profiling
  • ❌ No data collection: the developer collects no personal data from users
  • ❌ No upload to developer servers: your documents are never transmitted to our servers
  • ❌ No location data
  • ❌ No contacts / call log access
  • ❌ No microphone use

6. Crash reports and diagnostics

If you have agreed in your device settings to share diagnostic and crash data, platform operators (Apple, Google, Microsoft) may forward anonymised crash reports to the developer. These contain device model and OS version, technical error information (stack traces) and the time of the crash. They contain no document or scan content, no personal data, and no PINs, passwords or biometric data.

7. Data deletion

  • Single documents: delete individual scans or folders directly in the app
  • All data: uninstall the app, which removes all locally stored data
  • Cloud sync data: delete the Loculix data in your cloud storage (iCloud, Google Drive, OneDrive) via the respective platform

8. Children

Loculix is not directed at children under 16 (Art. 8 GDPR). The app contains no content designed for children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Google Play COPPA compliance: This app is not directed at children under 13 and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

9. International users and data transfer

Because all data is processed and stored locally on your device, there is generally no cross-border data transfer. If you use the optional cloud sync, the privacy terms of your chosen cloud provider apply regarding storage location.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21) and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77).

Since Loculix stores no personal data on developer servers, most of these rights practically relate to your local data, which you manage yourself.

Questions about exercising your rights: info@loculix.de

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via app updates (release notes) or on the website. The current version is always available at www.loculix.de/app-datenschutz.
