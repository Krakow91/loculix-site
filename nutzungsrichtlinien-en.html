Acceptable Use Policy

As of April 2026

This policy supplements our Terms (German) and Privacy Policy. It describes how the Loculix app and website should be used responsibly.

1. Intended use

Loculix is a tool for personal document management. The app is designed to digitise, organise and securely store private and business documents (invoices, contracts, insurance policies, notices, etc.).

  • Scanning and storing your own documents
  • Using automatic OCR text recognition and metadata extraction
  • Organising documents in virtual folders
  • Using full-text search
  • Optionally using encrypted cloud sync

2. Prohibited use

You agree not to use the app or website for the following purposes:

2.1 Unlawful content

  • Storing, processing or distributing content that violates applicable law
  • Managing documents related to criminal acts
  • Storing child sexual abuse material or any other criminally relevant material

2.2 Technical manipulation

  • Reverse engineering, decompiling or disassembling the app (unless permitted by law)
  • Circumventing or disabling security mechanisms (e.g. encryption, PIN protection)
  • Introducing malware, viruses or other harmful code
  • Automated use of the app or website by bots, scrapers or similar tools

2.3 Abuse of the website

  • Sending spam, advertising or unsolicited messages via the contact form
  • Multiple waitlist signups with fake email addresses
  • Attempting to overload the website or servers (DDoS)
  • Exploiting vulnerabilities without prior notice to the provider (see section 6)

3. User responsibility

You are solely responsible for:

  • Your content: All documents and data you store in the app
  • Backups: Create regular backups of your important documents. The provider is not liable for data loss
  • Credentials: Keep your PIN, password and recovery key safe. If lost, the provider cannot restore access to your encrypted data
  • Device security: Keep your operating system and the app up to date

⚠️ Important: Because Loculix follows the “privacy by design” principle and stores no user data on its own servers, recovering lost credentials (PIN, password, recovery key) is technically impossible. Please act accordingly.

4. Automatic metadata is no substitute for professional advice

Loculix automatically extracts information from scanned documents (e.g. amounts, contract numbers, deadlines, categories). This information is generated by OCR and AI analysis and may be incorrect or incomplete.

  • It is not legal, tax or financial advice
  • It does not replace manual review by the user
  • It should always be verified against the original document, especially for safety-critical or time-sensitive matters

5. Use by minors

Loculix is intended for persons aged 16 and over. Minors under 16 may only use the app and website with the consent of a legal guardian. The provider assumes no responsibility for use by minors without such consent.

6. Reporting security vulnerabilities (responsible disclosure)

If you discover a security vulnerability in the app or on the website, please report it responsibly:

  • Send an email to info@loculix.de with the subject “Security report”
  • Describe the issue in as much detail as possible
  • Do not exploit or publish the vulnerability before it has been fixed

We appreciate every report and will fix vulnerabilities as quickly as possible.

7. Violations and consequences

In case of violations the provider reserves the right to:

  • Send a warning by email (if the address is known)
  • Block access to the waitlist or contact form
  • Revoke the usage licence in serious cases
  • Take legal action for criminally relevant violations

8. Changes to this policy

The provider may change this policy at any time with effect for the future. The current version is available on the website. Significant changes will be announced in the app or on the website where technically possible.
