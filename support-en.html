Support

Help for Loculix

For problems, questions or feedback, email info@loculix.de or use the contact form. Please include your device model, operating system and app version. Do not send documents or passwords.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my documents stored?

Only locally on your device, encrypted with AES-256. The developer has no access.

I forgot my PIN or password.

The encryption key is derived from your PIN or password and is never stored unencrypted. Without it, recovery is not possible, not even by me. Keep your credentials and regular backups safe.

How does syncing across devices work?

Optionally via iCloud, Google Drive, OneDrive or WebDAV. Data is end-to-end encrypted on your device before upload.

How do I delete my data?

Delete single scans or folders in the app. Uninstalling removes all local data. Delete sync data in your cloud storage.

Is there a subscription or can I get a refund?

Loculix is a one-time purchase without a subscription. Refunds are handled by the respective store (Apple, Google, Microsoft).

Text recognition made a mistake.

OCR and extracted metadata are suggestions and may contain errors. Please verify important details such as deadlines and amounts yourself. Feel free to send me an example without sensitive content.

Which permissions does the app need?

Camera (scanning), photos (import), optionally notifications and biometrics. See the app privacy policy.
